# Roadmap

This document outlines the planned direction of the
`@ailura/nestjs-hono-adapter` project for the next 12 months
(2026).

## Goals

### Primary Goals

1. **Maintain compatibility** with NestJS 11, 12, and future
   versions
2. **Keep the bundle small** — stay under 10KB for the main
   entrypoint
3. **Comprehensive test coverage** — achieve 90%+ statement
   coverage
4. **Complete documentation** — ensure all features are
   documented

### Secondary Goals

1. **Performance benchmarks** — publish regular benchmark
   results
2. **Community growth** — lower the barrier for new contributors
3. **Ecosystem integration** — ensure compatibility with popular
   Hono middleware

## What We Are Not

- We are not a web framework — we are an adapter
- We do not plan to support Express or Fastify
- We do not plan to add features outside the
  `AbstractHttpAdapter` contract
